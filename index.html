1984

George Orwell

Set in a totalitarian future society, this novel follows Winston Smith as he struggles against constant surveillance, censorship, and the oppressive rule of Big Brother. A chilling warning about power and control.

The Secret History

Donna Tartt

At an elite college in New England, a group of classics students becomes entangled in a dangerous secret that leads to murder. A haunting exploration of beauty, obsession, and morality.

The Night Circus

Erin Morgenstern

A mysterious circus appears without warning, opening only at night. Behind its magic, two young illusionists are bound to a fierce competition that will change their lives forever.

Pride and Prejudice

Jane Austen

The story of Elizabeth Bennet and the proud Mr. Darcy navigating manners, morality, and marriage in Regency England. Austen's wit and social commentary remain unmatched.

The Shadow of the Wind

Carlos Ruiz Zafón

In post-war Barcelona, a young boy discovers a forgotten novel that draws him into a mystery of lost authors, hidden secrets, and dangerous intrigue.

Dracula

Bram Stoker

When Count Dracula travels from Transylvania to England, a group of determined individuals must confront the ancient vampire before darkness spreads across the land.

Frankenstein

Mary Shelley

A brilliant scientist creates life through an ambitious experiment, only to be haunted by the consequences of his creation. A powerful tale of ambition, responsibility, and humanity.
